# Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fairy Tutor ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Fairy Tutor mobile application and related services (the "App").

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

### 1.1 Account and Profile Information

- Name
- Email address
- Authentication details needed to sign in
- Class, grade, board, language preference, and academic profile details

### 1.2 Learning and Usage Information

- Study questions asked in the App
- Homework submissions and generated answers
- Conversation history
- Exam activity, answers, scores, report cards, and progress data
- Subject selections, preferences, and in-app settings

### 1.3 Uploaded Content

- Images uploaded for study or homework
- PDF, DOC, and DOCX files uploaded in supported flows
- Text extracted from uploaded files

### 1.4 Device and Technical Information

- App logs and error data
- Basic device and app usage information required for app stability, security, and debugging

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Create and manage user accounts
- Personalize learning experience based on class, board, subject, and language
- Answer study questions and generate homework help
- Process uploaded images and documents
- Save conversation history and allow users to continue previous sessions
- Generate exam results, report cards, and progress tracking
- Improve app quality, performance, and safety
- Detect misuse, abuse, fraud, or technical issues

## 3. AI-Generated Responses

Fairy Tutor uses AI-powered systems to generate explanations, translations, study responses, homework help, narration, and visual content.

- AI responses may be incomplete, incorrect, or differently phrased than a textbook or teacher explanation.
- Users should verify important academic content with teachers, official syllabus material, or trusted educational resources.

## 4. Translation Notice

If a user chooses a language other than English, some answers may be translated by AI.

- AI-generated translations may occasionally phrase words differently or not fully match the intended meaning.
- Users should reconfirm unclear or important content with their teacher or syllabus.

## 5. How Information Is Stored

We may store data using services such as:

- Firebase Authentication
- Cloud Firestore
- Firebase Storage or other secure file storage
- Application servers and supporting APIs used to process study, homework, and exam requests

We take reasonable steps to protect stored data, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

## 6. How Information May Be Shared

We do not sell personal information.

We may share information only as needed with:

- Cloud and infrastructure providers supporting the App
- AI and speech service providers used to generate answers, narration, or translations
- Service providers helping us store files, authenticate users, or run the App
- Legal or regulatory authorities when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security

Shared data is limited to what is reasonably necessary for the service to function.

## 7. Children and Student Use

Fairy Tutor is built for students and educational use. If the App is used by children, guardians, schools, or parents should review usage appropriately.

If you believe a child has provided personal information in a way that should be removed, contact us and we will review the request.

## 8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to:

- Provide the App
- Maintain conversation history and academic progress
- Meet legal, security, operational, and dispute-resolution needs

We may delete or anonymize data when it is no longer required.

## 9. User Choices

Depending on how the App is implemented, users may be able to:

- Update profile details
- Change language and academic preferences
- Delete or replace uploaded files
- Request deletion of account-related data

## 10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However:

- no app or server can guarantee absolute security
- users should avoid uploading highly sensitive personal information unless necessary for the learning flow

## 11. Third-Party Services

The App may rely on third-party tools and services for:

- authentication
- cloud storage
- AI response generation
- text-to-speech
- analytics, logging, or error monitoring

Those third parties may process data under their own terms and privacy practices.

## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we may update the effective date above. Continued use of the App after updates means the updated policy will apply unless otherwise required by law.
